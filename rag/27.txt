Pharmacy Take-Home Messages                                                                      19




      Lipid solubility influences extraction, formulation and biological distribution.


      Glycerol is the core alcohol of triglycerides and has medical applications.


      Unsaturation affects melting point and oxidative stability.


      Lipases hydrolyze triglycerides; NaOH causes saponification.


      Waxes are useful in topical and ointment preparations.




KEY POINT    Always connect chemical structure → physical property → pharmaceutical relevance.
