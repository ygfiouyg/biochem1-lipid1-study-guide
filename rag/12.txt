Saturated vs Unsaturated Fatty Acids                                                                                         09




    Saturated                                                                    Unsaturated
    No C=C double bonds                                                          One or more C=C double bonds

    Example: Butyric acid                                                        Monounsaturated: one double bond
    4 carbons; found mainly in butter                                            Polyunsaturated: more than one



             CH₃–CH₂–CH₂–COOH                                                     Examples: oleic • linoleic • arachidonic




KEY POINT    Unsaturation introduces bends and generally lowers melting point.
