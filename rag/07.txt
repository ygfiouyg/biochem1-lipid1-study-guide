III. Derived Lipids: Products of Hydrolysis                                                                          04




     Hydrolysis of simple or compound lipids yields smaller lipid-derived components.




            Fatty acids                                   Alcohols                             Hydrocarbons
      Saturated or unsaturated                   Glycerol or higher alcohols                  Hydrocarbon products




KEY POINT    Hydrolysis breaks lipid molecules into fatty acids, alcohols and hydrocarbons.
